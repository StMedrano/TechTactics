# Responsible Use and Compliance Notes

This project is designed for targeted, human-reviewed business development rather than indiscriminate bulk outreach.

## Required operating practices
- Follow Google Maps Platform terms, attribution requirements, and any applicable Places data storage/caching restrictions.
- Follow applicable email, text-message, telemarketing, privacy, and consumer-protection rules for the contact method and jurisdiction you choose.
- Honor opt-outs and do not repeatedly contact businesses that decline.
- Do not misrepresent the TechTactics concept preview as the prospect's official website.
- Do not fabricate reviews, performance measurements, business facts, certifications, or endorsements.
- Do not use protected logos/images without permission.
- Keep API keys and prospect data out of Git.
- Review all model-generated claims before using them externally.

## V1 safeguard
The software itself does not send outreach. It generates drafts and requires a separate human approval state before a lead can even be marked as contacted.
