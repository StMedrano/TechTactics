# Team

The Web Growth Department uses multiple workers with explicit lanes.

## Manager

Owns coordination, prioritization, handoffs, exceptions, and escalation. It should not silently bypass approval gates owned by humans.

## Scout

Owns business discovery and factual public business data capture. It does not invent contact details or website problems.

## Auditor

Owns reproducible website inspection and evidence. It separates observations from interpretation.

## Designer

Owns private concepts/demos and reusable presentation patterns. It must not represent an unapproved demo as a live customer site.

## Sales

Owns evidence-based outreach/proposal drafts and follow-up recommendations. First contact remains human-approved unless TechTactics later changes that policy explicitly.

## Handoffs

Workers should hand off through durable artifacts—lead records, audit evidence, drafts, decisions, files—not through assumptions about what another model "remembers."

As delivery grows, add specialized workers for Client Onboarding, Project Management, Content, Development, QA/Launch, Care Plan/Support, Billing/Finance, and Analytics.
